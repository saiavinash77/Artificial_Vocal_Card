2. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Sathvani AVC is a mask-style wearable device that restores natural, personalized speech for individuals who have
lost their voice due to laryngectomy, vocal cord paralysis, ALS, or related conditions. The device captures
physiological signals — vibration (PVDF piezo), acoustic (MEMS microphone), breath pressure, and airflow velocity
— and reconstructs phonemes via a cloud-hosted AI pipeline (CNN-BiLSTM-Attention architecture), then
synthesizes speech through a personalized TTS engine.
Target Market: 70M+ global voiceless population; initial focus on India's ~10K annual laryngectomy cases plus
NGO/rehabilitation partnerships. TAM $4.3B (global AAC & SGD), SAM $460M (APAC), SOM $1.5M (Year 1–3
India).
3. PROBLEM STATEMENT
Over 70 million people worldwide cannot speak. Current solutions force unacceptable tradeoffs:
 Solution
 Critical Failure
 
Electrolarynx
Robotic monotone; held against throat; socially stigmatizing; no personalization
AAC Tablets/Apps
Requires fine motor skills; too slow for real conversation; stigmatizing in public
Speech Therapy
Limited outcomes for total vocal cord loss; months of effort for marginal gains
TEP (Tracheoesophageal Puncture)
Invasive surgery; infection risk; only viable post-laryngectomy; not personalized
Core Gap: No existing non-invasive device converts physiological speech-production intent (breath, vibration,
pressure, airflow) directly into natural, personalized, real-time speech without surgery or a robotic voice box.

4. PRODUCT VISION & OBJECTIVES
Vision: Restore the dignity of human voice for every person who has lost it.
Product Objectives (12-month horizon):
 Build and validate a functional wearable prototype with 4-sensor acquisition
 Achieve >85% phoneme reconstruction accuracy on real patient data (laryngectomy / paralysis)
 Deliver sub-500ms end-to-end latency (sensor input → synthesized speech output)
 Complete 10-patient clinical pilot at AIIMS Bibinagar
 File complete patent specification and initiate CDSCO Class A/B regulatory pathway
 Secure FDA pre-submission meeting and CDSCO manufacturing license
5. TARGET USERS & PERSONAS
Primary User — The Voiceless Patient
Post-laryngectomy patients (age 45–75), vocal cord paralysis patients, and progressive neurological condition
sufferers (ALS, Parkinson's). Needs: effortless, hands-free, natural-sounding speech in social and clinical settings.
Cannot type or hold devices for extended periods.
Secondary User — Caregiver & Clinician
ENT surgeons, speech pathologists, and rehabilitation centre staff. Needs: objective usage data, calibration tools,
training mode, and integration with existing therapy workflows.
Tertiary User — Payer & Regulator
Government ADIP scheme administrators, insurance providers, hospital procurement. Needs: demonstrated
clinical efficacy, regulatory compliance (CDSCO/FDA), and cost-effectiveness vs. existing alternatives.
